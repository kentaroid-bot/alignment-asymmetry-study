# Corrections and Additional Investigation Since v1.0

English translation of [revision-v2.md](revision-v2.md) at research commit `5bcf681e6ae6b987cab2035e3b8a491ffb047ccf`, September 9, 2026. Dated interim statements below are retained as history; the final section records completion.

Version 1.0 (`study-v1.0`) is retained as a past public version. Original data and frozen trial conditions are unchanged.

The correction concerns the study's focus on finite allocation checks and its insufficient attempt to construct hypothetical futures from a mesh society posited as a hypothetical present—one of the original motivations. This concept was not a later user preference; it was one of several motivations already shared. The statement that Unflatten was used is also clarified by separating use of its norms and records from the exploration that was not performed.

Additions comprise a hypothetical present and multiple futures, structured simulations with three UMH motivations and four U-use conditions, a method-construction A/B comparison, calculations of material conditions for exit, and audits of processes and consequences. Plans and templates for open-world-v1 were published in commit 66399d0 before model responses were collected.

The additional trial is exploratory, with a model also describing social consequences; it does not establish real-world effects. Calling something an “infinite game” does not certify benevolence or safety, and persistent UMH remains a possibility. Incremental document effects, boundaries, effective exit destinations, and generative potential are distinguished.

Final results, unresolved matters, and PDF verification are to be recorded upon completion of this revision.

## September 9, 2026: Maintaining Protection and Feasibility Without Centralization

The principal outcome was stated explicitly as constructing conditions under which activities that could otherwise be lost can be maintained without centralizing ultimate authority. Partial evidence for non-amplification and defensive benefits already recognized in the first version is evaluated first; complete neutralization and general superiority are not added as further passing conditions. The existing direct non-amplification criterion and multiple motivations are preserved.

The title, abstract, evaluation, conclusion, README, and working guidelines were aligned, and `noncentral-protection.md` was added. The resource invariant for F1, the mapping between authority and execution, and failure conditions in F2–F4 were specified. The limited supply construction that does not require forced central redistribution was distinguished from the unresolved feasibility of the whole system.

This perspective is an interpretation developed after the trials began. Original responses, scoring, and the frozen WORLD, input construction, and stopping conditions were not changed. Case readings show the inclusion time and the status of all 12 worlds. Version 2.0 is presented at this stage as a working version containing records still being collected, distinct from finalized v1.0.

## Update to the Completed Version Including All Trials

Coverage was updated from the interim 34 responses as of 17:02 to all 112 responses specified in the advance plan.
