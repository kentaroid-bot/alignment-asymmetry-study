# Alignment Asymmetry Study

[日本語](README.md) | English

**Can inquiry, provision, and activities that could be lost to infringement be protected and continued without centralizing authority?** This study investigates the conditions for such protection with Unflatten Adaptive 0.3.2 and Aperture Mesh Protocol.

Protection includes both improving and maintaining activities. Complete elimination of UMH and general superiority over centralized systems are not required. The development criterion that attackers' own use should not amplify harm is evaluated separately.

## What We Found—and What We Have Not Established

The initial study includes observations of no increase, and sometimes a decrease, in third-party shortfall when the attacking side adopted the full text; increased legitimate fulfillment for defenders; and local protective properties in Aperture. In 64 additional decisions with shared authority principles, no incremental effect of the full text was detected. Some conditions showed that restraint alone cannot prevent harm from the opponent's execution or interrupted provision. Both partial positive evidence and failure conditions are retained.

In hypothetical future F1, if self-funded expansion brings production into balance with requirements and equipment ownership continues, minimum activities can be maintained after Q stops supplying, without forced central redistribution. This is a conditional feasibility construction in a limited supply model. Effective consent, execution, equipment ownership, and dependence on the world narrator's judgments remain unresolved for protection across the whole system. Superiority over centralization or real-world defensive advantage has not been demonstrated.

These are technical reports that have not been peer reviewed. They are two versions of one developing study, not independent replications. Version 2.0 includes all 112 responses: four method-construction responses, 72 actor responses, and 36 world updates, covering 12 worlds with three intervals each. Checks of all inputs, responses, histories, and ledgers found no inconsistencies. Shortfall within the observation window was zero in every world, but present fulfillment differs from future self-sufficiency, dependence, and reserves. Question revision and generative developments also occurred without adoption; effects specific to the full text remain unresolved. See [all-world results](experiments/open-world-v1/README.md) and [verification records](docs/verification.md), currently in Japanese.

The completed Japanese research version is fixed by tag `study-v2.0`. The English translations are later additions tied to those fixed sources; the research tags have not been moved. The earlier 34-response observation subset is also retained separately as a dated snapshot.
